Tenant Town Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Tenant Town ("the game") is made by Shawn Riley ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts or photos.

Information stored on your device

Your town, progress, settings and a record of your purchases are saved only on your device. We do not receive a copy. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game which item you bought so it can be delivered, and the game keeps a record of the order on your device so it is never delivered twice.

Advertising

The iPhone and iPad version of the game contains no ads and collects no data. The Android version may offer optional video ads that you choose to watch for rewards. Those ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

On Android, if you buy the Ad-free Pass, no ads are shown and video rewards are given instantly.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example, when a building is finished). They are created on your device and nothing is sent to us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn reminders off in the game's Settings or your phone's settings.
  • Change ad consent in Settings > Privacy choices (where available).
  • Limit ad tracking in your phone's privacy settings.
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
